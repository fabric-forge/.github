**fabric-forge** is where [ry-ops](https://github.com/ry-ops) turns the infrastructure-as-a-fabric ideas from [git-fabric](https://github.com/git-fabric) into finished products: small, serverless, and mostly running on Cloudflare.

## Live

| | |
|---|---|
| **[ry-ops.dev](https://ry-ops.dev)** | The blog: infrastructure, homelab, automation, MCP, and the Infrastructure as a Fabric philosophy. Built with Astro 5. |
| **[DriveIQ](https://driveiq.ry-ops.dev)** | Vehicle maintenance tracking, service reminders and AI answers from your owner's manual, rebuilt serverless on Cloudflare Workers. The original lives at [ry-ops/DriveIQ](https://github.com/ry-ops/DriveIQ). |

The repositories here are private while the products evolve.
